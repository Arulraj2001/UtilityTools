<h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 1rem;">Expert Tips for Bank Exam Photos</h2>

<ul style="margin-left: 1.25rem; color: #333;">
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Professional Appearance:</strong> Banks value professionals. Wear what you'd wear to office. Shirt, tie, blazer all good.</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Good Grooming:</strong> Hair should be neat and combed. Facial hair should be clean (not scruffy). Overall appearance should be professional.</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Natural Lighting:</strong> Take photo in daylight near window or outside. Bad lighting = rejected photo.</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Straight Posture:</strong> Sit straight, don't lean. Look directly at camera with serious expression.</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Proper Distance:</strong> Photo should be from shoulders up, not too close (face touching camera) and not too far.</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Center Your Face:</strong> Face should be in middle of photo. Not tilted left or right. Not looking down or up.</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Recent Photo Preferred:</strong> Use photo taken within 3 months. Old photos from years back are not accepted.</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Print Quality Matters:</strong> Download from this tool and print on photo paper. Glossy photo paper looks most professional.</li>
</ul>
