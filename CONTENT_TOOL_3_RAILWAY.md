<h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 1rem;">Tips for Perfect Railway Photo</h2>

<ul style="margin-left: 1.25rem; color: #333;">
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Use White Background:</strong> Railways require white background. Use a white wall or white cloth behind you.</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Take in Good Light:</strong> Take photo in daylight or near a window. Dark photos are rejected.</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Look Straight at Camera:</strong> Head straight, eyes looking directly at camera. No looking sideways.</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Remove Glasses:</strong> Most Railways don't allow glasses. If you must wear glasses, they must be clear (no colored lenses).</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Neutral Expression:</strong> Smile is okay, but not a big grin. Keep a professional, calm look.</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>No Head Covering:</strong> No hats, caps, scarves on head. Unless for religious reasons (then it's allowed).</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Print on Good Quality Paper:</strong> After resizing, print on photo paper, not normal paper. Photo paper makes it look professional.</li>
</ul>
