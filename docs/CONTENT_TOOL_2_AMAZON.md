<h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 1rem;">What is Amazon Fee Calculator?</h2>

<p style="margin-bottom: 1rem; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;">
<strong>Amazon Fee Calculator</strong> is a free tool that tells you exactly how much money Amazon will take from your sales. If you sell on Amazon (even just a few items), this tool is a MUST.
</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 1rem; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;">
When you sell something on Amazon, the company takes money for:
- Selling commission (15% on average)
- Shipping cost (if you use FBA)
- Storage fees (space they use in their warehouse)
- Referral fees
- Other charges

Our calculator adds all these up so you know your real profit BEFORE you make the sale.
</p>

<h3 style="font-size: 1.1rem; font-weight: 600; margin: 1.5rem 0 0.75rem 0;">Why This Matters</h3>
<p style="margin-bottom: 1rem; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;">
Many new sellers think: "I'll sell this item for Rs. 1000 and make Rs. 800 profit!" But then Amazon takes Rs. 250 in fees and the seller is left with only Rs. 200. This tool helps you avoid this surprise.
</p>
