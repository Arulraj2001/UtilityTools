<h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 1rem;">Pro Tips for Best Results</h2>

<ul style="margin-left: 1.25rem; color: #333;">
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Good Lighting is Important:</strong> Take photo in daylight or bright room. Dark photo will be rejected.</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>White or Light Background:</strong> SSC prefers white or very light background. No colored backgrounds.</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Formal Dress:</strong> Wear shirt, kurta, or saree like you'd wear to office. This is official document.</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Clear Face:</strong> Face should be clearly visible, not covered. No sunglasses or heavy shades.</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Recent Photo:</strong> Use photo taken within last 3 months. Old or outdated looking photos are not accepted.</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>No Filters:</strong> Don't use Instagram filters or Snapchat effects. Photo must look like real you.</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Print on Good Paper:</strong> After downloading, print on photo paper. Never print on normal paper. It looks unprofessional.</li>

<li style="margin-bottom: 0.75rem;"><strong>Keep a Copy:</strong> Keep soft copy (digital) and hard copy (printed) both. Useful if needed later.</li>
