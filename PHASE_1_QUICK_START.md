## ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Don't:**
- Write thin content (<300 words per page)
- Copy content from competitors (plagiarism)
- Stuff keywords unnaturally
- Forget internal linking
- Neglect mobile optimization
- Skip FAQ sections
- Ignore meta data
- Forget to submit sitemap

✅ **Do:**
- Write original, expert content
- Follow templates provided
- Include real examples
- Link related content
- Test on mobile
- Use schema markup
- Optimize meta tags
- Submit to Search Console

## 💡 CONTENT WRITING TIPS

### Make Content "Valuable"
- Include specific numbers/data
- Provide step-by-step guides
- Share real examples
- Explain the "why" not just "how"
- Address common problems
- Offer pro tips

### Make Content "Original"
- Use your own examples
- Add your unique perspective
- Include real use cases
- Personalize with your experience
- Reference your tools/products
- Don't copy competitors

### Make Content "Long-Form"
- Minimum 500 words per tool page
- Minimum 1500 words per blog post
- Multiple sections with subheadings
- Comprehensive coverage of topic
- FAQ with 7+ questions
- Internal links (3-5 per page)
