<h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 1rem;">What is Volumetric Weight Calculator?</h2>

<p style="margin-bottom: 1rem; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;">
<strong>Volumetric Weight Calculator</strong> is a free online tool that helps you calculate the "volumetric weight" of your packages. If you ship items using courier services like FedEx, UPS, DHL, or Amazon, you need this tool.
</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 1rem; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;">
Volumetric weight (also called "dimensional weight" or "DIM weight") is how courier companies measure large packages that are light. For example, if you're shipping a pillow or a cardboard box full of soft items, it takes up a lot of space but weighs very little. The courier company charges you based on space, not just weight.
</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 1rem; line-height: 1.75; color: #333;">
Our calculator makes this super easy. Just enter three numbers (length, width, height), and it tells you the volumetric weight instantly. No math needed!
</p>
